| **VegPlotter** | https://www.vegplotter.com/ | Digital garden planner |
| **Planta** | planta.im | Houseplant care (subscription) |

### Key Observation

Most existing AI gardening tools focus on **visual/aesthetic garden design** or **houseplant identification** — not food production guidance. None are open-source. None combine AI Q&A, planting calendars, plant diagnosis, and community food sharing in a free platform.

**This is the gap OpenHarvest fills.**
